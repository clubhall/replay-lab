## Concept prompt

Create a professional product design concept screenshot for CLUBHall Replay, a browser sports video watching app, 1536 x 1024 landscape. This is an implementation specification, with all future interactive typography and controls implemented as HTML. A quietly futuristic, premium sports cinema. Warm off-white canvas (#f4f1ea), black graphite typography, finely machined champagne accents, clean sans-serif with a large editorial serif heading used only inside the empty video. Header 90px high: left CLUBHall with REPLAY underneath, centered navigation Watch / Moments / Practice, right simple gold 'Open match' button and small circular avatar A. Main content grid has generous 40px margins: LEFT 70% wide, RIGHT 27%. LEFT: small title row 'Your match, reimagined.' and tiny text 'TENNIS · REPLAY'. Below a widescreen 16:9 cinematic media surface with a beautiful photographic Rio tennis court at sunset (reference court mood), discreet radius 18px, shadow; inside centered readable white heading 'Every match has a story.' and smaller 'Bring yours into focus.' then an ivory 'Choose a video' button. Small lower-left label 'COURTSIDE · CONCEPT SCENE' makes it clear it is not loaded match footage. Below image a compact actual control bar disabled until video upload: play icon, -5s, +5s, 0:00 / --:--, a thin seek track, 1x, loop icon, fullscreen. Below this a title row 'Your moments' and '+ Save moment', a refined empty filmstrip region with one add tile and text 'The points you want to come back to.' 'Save a moment while you watch.' No fake thumbnails, no fake metrics. RIGHT column: the golden arena companion occupies a clearly distinct architectural environment, a tall sculptural alcove with a curved pale limestone back wall and floor, soft upper warm skylight, foreground threshold lip, and a floating golden rounded rectangle stadium bowl with hollow center (keep supplied reference arena form recognizable); floor receives a shadow so arena floats within its OWN ROOM. The room is divided by a deliberate reveal from surrounding UI, no cheap card shadow or glowing orb. Small 'ARENA' etching near lower wall. Under room, label 'YOUR REPLAY COMPANION', title 'A little closer to your game.' copy 'Save a moment. Slow it down. See it differently.' Two clean line-separated actions 'Find a moment' with bookmark icon and 'Review in slow motion' with dial icon. Bottom tiny quiet progress line 'Your first replay starts here.' No score, no win rates, no AI analysis claims. Complete screen composition, fully readable typography and carefully aligned controls, restrained not cluttered. No browser frame, no device mockup. Reference images show ONLY brand arena geometry and court mood; do not copy their original layouts. Preserve real product flow and these exact key labels.
